# Camera based Parking Guidance System (PGS)
#### Client Name: Anis Mohammed

It is required to develop a camera based parking guidance system similar to the systems available in the market.

- The product incorporate 2 cameras to view the upto 6 car parking spots - 3 from one camera and 3 from the other.
- All camera related features like zoom, hue, saturation will be controlled by client's software. Product just needs to pass the stream without latency and quality loss.
- The resolution of the camera should be enough as to incorporate LPR system.
- High brightness LED should be given as to indicate in different scenarios to the user.
- The stream will be passed through ethernet interface.
- The camera will be PoE powered. The PoE configuration will be used in cascaded form as to further connect multiple PGS system.
- The product will have a decent powered speaker as well.
